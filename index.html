Nova Horizons Management Consultancy L.L.C

Nova Horizons Management Consultancy L.L.C is a UAE-registered management consultancy company providing professional advisory and consulting services to corporate clients, investors, and institutions operating within the United Arab Emirates, Africa, and selected international markets.

The company operates strictly within its licensed consultancy activities and does not engage in trading, freight forwarding, logistics operations, or handling of goods. All services rendered are non-operational, advisory, and strategic in nature.

Nova Horizons serves as a strategic advisory platform, supporting clients with planning, analysis, structuring, and execution support across regulated and emerging markets.
